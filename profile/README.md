# SwiftGuard

**SwiftGuard is the AI receptionist for plumbing and heating businesses.** It picks up the phone when the plumber cannot: the emergency at half past eleven at night, the call that rings while both hands are under a sink, the one that comes in halfway through a boiler service. SwiftGuard takes the caller's details, books the appointment into the calendar, and follows up afterwards to ask for a review.

A business decides how much of the phone to hand over. **All calls** makes SwiftGuard the front door. **Only when you can't** keeps the handset ringing first, and SwiftGuard steps in when nobody picks up, the line is busy, or the phone is off. Either way the business keeps its own number: forwarding is one short code dialled on the existing handset, undone just as quickly.

## Who builds it

SwiftGuard is built and run by [Daan van den Bergh](https://swiftguard.ai/about) from Bilthoven, the Netherlands, and launched in 2026. It serves plumbing and heating businesses across Europe; call forwarding is available for Dutch phone lines today, with more countries to follow.
